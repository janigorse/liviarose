Return Policy

Policy Overview

LiviaRose specializes in handmade hair clips crafted individually by artisan Olivia. Because each item is custom-made with care and attention, we operate under a no-returns and no-refunds policy for all purchases.

Key Points:
  • All sales are final
  • No returns accepted after purchase
  • No refunds issued for any reason
  • This policy applies to all products and orders

Why We Have This Policy

Our no-return policy reflects the unique nature of handmade products:

  • Custom Creation: Each hair clip is individually handcrafted specifically for the customer. Once created and shipped, it cannot be resold to another customer.
  • Quality Guarantee: All products are carefully inspected for quality before shipment. We take pride in creating durable, beautiful clips.
  • Artisan Work: Handmade items represent the artisan's time, skill, and materials. Once completed, they cannot be reconstructed or reused.
  • Hygiene: Hair accessories require close contact with the scalp. For health and safety reasons, used clips cannot be resold or returned to inventory.

What About Defective Items?

While extremely rare, if you receive a product with a manufacturing defect (such as broken materials, defective clasps, or structural damage due to our workmanship), we may, at our sole discretion, offer a replacement or store credit.

To report a defect:

  1. Contact us within 14 days of receiving your order at info@liviarose.shop
  2. Provide clear photos of the defect
  3. Describe the issue in detail
  4. We will evaluate and determine if a replacement or credit is appropriate
Important: Damage from misuse, wear and tear, or user modification is NOT covered. Defects must be related to manufacturing and reported promptly.

Damaged Shipments

If your item arrives damaged due to carrier damage (not our packaging), please contact us immediately with photos. Carrier-related damage claims must be filed within the shipping provider's timeframe (typically 24-48 hours).

Product Information

About Our Hair Clips

LiviaRose handmade hair clips are creative accessories designed for holding hair in place. They are made using quality materials selected for durability and comfort.

Product Characteristics

  • Handmade: Each clip is individually created by artisan Olivia
  • Unique: No two clips are exactly alike
  • Colors: Wide variety of colors available; customers receive hand-picked selections
  • Materials: Quality craft materials (specifics vary by design)
  • Durability: Designed for regular use and long-lasting wear

Care Instructions

To extend the life of your hair clips:

  • Store in a clean, dry place away from excessive heat
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight, which may fade colors
  • Handle with care to avoid bending or damage
  • Clean gently with a dry cloth as needed
  • Keep away from liquids and harsh chemicals
  • Not recommended for very fine or very thick hair types that may cause excessive strain

Age Appropriateness

LiviaRose hair clips are designed for children ages 5 and up, typically 5-12 years old. Always supervise young children with hair accessories. Clips should not be used by children under 3 years old.

Surprise Element

When you order, you receive hand-picked selections of colors and designs chosen by Olivia specifically for you. This is intentional and is part of the LiviaRose experience. You cannot request specific colors or designs for custom orders. By placing an order, you accept this surprise element.
